The Doctrine of Forgiveness Through Jesus.

Brought to by "The Kellys' Loyal Bible study and exploration Network together with "The Kellys' Loyal Family at: http://thekellysloyalfamily.netlify.app

Forgiveness through Jesus Christ is the heart of the Gospel. It means our sins are not counted against us because Jesus paid the price through His blood. To understand this, we need to look at two important words from the Bible: Expiation and Propitiation.

What is Expiation?

Expiation refers to the removal or cleansing of sin. Sin creates a stain and a record of guilt in the spiritual realm. God is holy and cannot overlook sin. Therefore, a price must be paid to cleanse this guilt. In the Old Testament, this was done through animal sacrifices:

“Without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness of sins.” (Hebrews 9:22)

The blood of animals symbolized life being given in place of the sinner. This is why witches and some cultures also use blood sacrifices. They understand a spiritual principle: the wages of sin is death (Romans 6:23). So, a life must stand in for another life. However, animal blood could never permanently remove sin (Hebrews 10:4).

What is Propitiation?

Propitiation means turning away wrath or satisfying justice. Sin provokes the wrath of God because He is righteous. When a sacrifice is offered, it appeases justice. In traditional beliefs, when people sacrifice a goat or chicken, they believe they have appeased spirits or gods. The blood of those animals stands in place for their sins or tressgressions so that the spirits won't see their sins and torment them again but instead see the blood of those sacrificed beings. But these are shadows of the ultimate reality revealed in Christ and His blood that stands in our place of our sins to God.

“He is the propitiation for our sins, and not for ours only but also for the sins of the whole world.” (1 John 2:2)

How Jesus Fulfilled It All

Jesus came as the perfect Lamb of God (John 1:29). His blood did what animal sacrifices and human efforts could never do:

  • He removed our guilt (Expiation): “Behold, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world.” (John 1:29)
  • He satisfied God’s justice (Propitiation): Romans 3:25 says God put Jesus forward as a propitiation by His blood.
  • No more sacrifices: “We have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.” (Hebrews 10:10)

Spiritual Law and Forgiveness

In the spiritual realm, sin demands a payment. Either you pay with your own life (eternal separation from God) the so called death, or someone pays for you because the laws of the spiritual realm is that when you sin, you have to die and in this, no one has the power to forgive without expiation and Propitiation. In this, Jesus became our substitute; All our sins of those in Christ are transferred to Jesus Christ because He suffered our in our place and on His blood we find freedom. When you believe in Him, His blood speaks on your behalf.

“There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.” (Romans 8:1)

Summary

All cultures know a life must pay for a life—that is why sacrifices exist. But in Christ, the ultimate sacrifice was made once and for all. If you are in Christ, no more blood sacrifice is needed. Forgiveness is complete in Him.

Jesus is our Plea. His Blood speaks better things than the blood of animals (Hebrews 12:24).
